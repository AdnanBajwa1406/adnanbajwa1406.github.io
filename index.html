About Me

I have acquired extensive expertise in research, teaching, and lab management. On the research front, I have significant experience in condensed matter physics and materials chemistry, including the synthesis of morphology-controlled bio-carbon, transition metal oxides, and composites for electromagnetic wave absorption with ultrawide bandwidth.


Additionally, I work on electrochemical CO2 reduction using solid-oxide electrolysis cells (SOECs), electrochemical oxygen evolution (OER), hydrogen evolution reactions (HER), and supercapacitor electrode design. During my recent Postdoctoral research, I have been co-supervising Master's students conducting SOEC/SOFC and electrochemical water-splitting research.


I also actively serve as a peer reviewer for top-tier journals, including Journal of Alloys and Compounds, Ceramics International, RSC Advances, and Applied Surface Science.

Current research on double perovskite-based reversible solid oxide cells and electrochemical CO2 reduction at Shenzhen University
